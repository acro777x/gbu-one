# App facts (source of truth)

The page may claim only what is listed under "Safe to claim". Everything else needs your confirmation first.

## Safe to claim (from the system specification)

- GBU One is a student companion app for Gautam Buddha University, Greater Noida. Android package `in.gbu.one`.
- Eight schools: SOICT, SOE, SOBT, SOM, SOLJG, SOHSS, SOVSAS, plus central administration.
- Offline-first: timetable, saved notices and opened papers are stored on the phone.
- Timetable: day view Monday to Friday, subject code, room, faculty, lecture type, amber badge when a room is shifted, optional alarm 10 minutes before class.
- Notices: filter by school, category tags, search, urgent pinned notices, PDF attachments cached for offline reading.
- PYQ library: 841+ subjects, filter by exam type (Mid Sem, End Sem, Back Paper), year, school, programme. In-app PDF viewer.
- Upload papers as a guest or student. Papers go to a review queue. An approved paper earns 50 points.
- Leaderboard: top 3 podium with gold, silver and bronze crowns. The exam cell admin holds the top spot at 1,000 points by default. 21 approved papers (1,050 points) overtakes it.
- Exam hub: date sheet, countdown to next exam, results watch.
- Academic calendar and campus events.
- Webmail helper opens the GBU webmail portal in an isolated view. The app never asks for, stores or sends the university password.
- Guest mode with an auto-generated ID like GBU-78A4B2. Points and uploads transfer to an account when the student signs up.
- 19 offline vector avatars across the schools.
- Light theme by default, dark theme supported.
- Admin tools are in a separate app (`in.gbu.one.admin`) and are not in the student APK.
- Compulsory update prompt when a required version is published.
- Tested: 38 test suites, 626 tests passing (only mention in a small technical note, not in the hero).
