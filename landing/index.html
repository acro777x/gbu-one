Frequently asked questions

Is this the official GBU app?
GBU One is an independent student companion project built for Gautam Buddha University students. It is not operated or officially endorsed by Gautam Buddha University administration.
Does it cost anything?
No. GBU One is completely free and open for all students. There are no fees, subscriptions, or locked features.
Does it need internet?
Only to fetch updates. Timetable, saved circulars, and previous year papers you opened work completely offline even in basement labs.
Is it safe?
It never asks for your university password. Admin tools are not in the student app. You can verify the APK integrity against the SHA-256 checksum printed above.
Why isn't it on the Play Store?
Direct APK distribution allows immediate updates without multi-week store review delays during exam season, and keeps the project independent and free without developer account fees.
Is there an iPhone version?
GBU One is Android only for now. An iOS version is under development; TestFlight availability will be posted once testing opens.
I found a wrong room or notice. Who do I tell?
Send corrections, missing timetable updates, or question paper scans to info@gbu.ac.in, or to the GBU One maintainers at student.gbuone@gmail.com.
